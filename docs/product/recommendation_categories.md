# Recommendation Categories

Categories are conclusions about current marginal giving opportunities, not permanent scores.

- **Top charity:** unusually strong evidence, implementation, cost-effectiveness, and room for more funding, with uncertainty unlikely to overturn the decision.
- **Recommended:** credible positive marginal impact and funding capacity, though not as strong or robust as `Top charity`.
- **Promising but uncertain:** plausible high impact with important unresolved evidence, implementation, or funding uncertainty.
- **Strong program, limited funding gap:** evidence/implementation may be attractive, but additional donations are unlikely to add comparable activity now.
- **Not currently recommended:** assessed opportunity does not meet the current threshold; explain whether this reflects evidence, cost, implementation, harm, or funding constraints.
- **Insufficient evidence:** available information cannot support a responsible conclusion.
- **Potential concern:** credible risk of material harm, severe governance/data issue, or misleading impact claim warrants donor caution.
- **Not evaluated:** no completed review; mandatory for Phase 2 profiles.

No category is assigned from financial transparency, overhead, brand, or intervention evidence alone. Categories expire on the review’s stated date.
