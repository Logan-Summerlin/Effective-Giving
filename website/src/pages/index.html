Find charities that make the most of every dollar.

ClearView evaluates U.S. charities based on evidence, cost-effectiveness, and marginal impact — not reputation or administrative ratios. We ask one question: what does your next donated dollar actually accomplish?

Our Evaluation Framework
Marginal Impact

We analyze what additional donations would accomplish — not what a charity has historically done. An already fully-funded charity may have low marginal impact even if its programs are excellent.

Evidence Quality

We grade every intervention type on a spectrum from Strong to Potentially Harmful. We read the studies, check causal identification, and report uncertainty honestly.

Cost-Effectiveness

We estimate expected social benefit per marginal dollar, with transparent assumptions, uncertainty ranges, and sensitivity analysis. We separate human welfare benefits from government fiscal savings.

Transparency

Financial and impact transparency are different things. A charity can file complete tax forms and still provide little information about whether its programs improve lives.

Credibility Standards
Conflict of Interest Policy

Staff, funders, and advisors must disclose any relationship with reviewed charities. This policy will be published before any recommendations go live.

Public Correction Log

Every factual error correction is logged publicly with the date, page, error description, and whether the correction changed a recommendation.

Charity Response Period

Before publishing a full review, the charity receives a response period to correct factual errors. Responses and unresolved disagreements are published in the report.

Reproducible Methods

All cost-effectiveness calculations and ranking code are published with full assumptions, uncertainty ranges, and sensitivity analysis so outside researchers can audit the conclusions.
